About OVHcloud

OVHcloud (legally OVH Groupe SA) is Europe's leading cloud provider, founded in 1999 by Octave Klaba in Roubaix, France. What started as a budget web hosting company with borrowed money has grown into one of the world's largest cloud infrastructure providers - operating 450,000+ servers across 46 data centres on 4 continents, serving 1.6 million customers in over 140 countries.

OVHcloud's defining advantage is vertical integration. The company designs its own servers, builds and owns all its data centres, and operates its own global fibre-optic backbone - a model that gives it exceptional cost control and the ability to offer some of the most competitive pricing in the global market. Its proprietary water-cooling technology (developed in 2003) gives it an unusually low PUE, making it one of the greenest large-scale cloud providers.

For Indian customers, OVHcloud launched its first Mumbai data centre in March 2023, building on a Mumbai Point of Presence it had operated since 2020. This gives Indian users access to OVHcloud's full product range - VPS, Public Cloud, Managed Kubernetes, GPU instances (H100, A100), Bare Metal servers, and Hosted Private Cloud - from Indian infrastructure, with lower latency and better compliance posture than routing to European or US regions.

OVHcloud is the natural choice for Indian teams that need a globally recognised European provider with local India presence - particularly for workloads involving European data partners, GDPR compliance, or teams that want the breadth of a global hyperscaler at a fraction of AWS/GCP pricing. Its open-source philosophy (OpenStack for Public Cloud, VMware/Nutanix for Private Cloud) also makes it attractive to teams avoiding vendor lock-in.

What's good about OVHcloud

  • Europe's largest cloud - globally recognised brand with 25+ years of infrastructure track record
  • VPS from just $4.20/mo - among the most affordable global providers for equivalent specs, with unlimited traffic included
  • Mumbai data centre (launched 2023) - Indian users get local latency without routing via Singapore or Europe
  • H100 and A100 GPU instances on Public Cloud - 99.99% uptime SLA, compatible with CUDA, PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX
  • Anti-DDoS protection included on all plans - no extra charge
  • No traffic billing on most plans (except Asia-Pacific region) - significant cost saving for data-heavy workloads
  • Global data centre network (46 sites, 4 continents) - multi-region deployments, disaster recovery, and low latency worldwide
  • Open infrastructure - OpenStack Public Cloud, VMware/Nutanix Private Cloud, managed Kubernetes, no proprietary lock-in
  • $200 free Public Cloud trial credit - no upfront commitment required to evaluate the platform
  • Proprietary water-cooling - industry-leading PUE, strong sustainability credentials, ISO 50001 energy management certified
  • ISO 27001, ISO 27701, HDS, SecNumCloud (French ANSSI) certified - among the most comprehensive compliance portfolios of any cloud
  • GDPR-compliant by design - European data sovereignty, suitable for teams with European regulatory requirements
⚠️

Watch-outs for Indian teams

  • USD billing, not INR - no rupee pricing; currency risk and no UPI/NEFT payment options
  • Traffic is billed in the Asia-Pacific region (including Mumbai) - unlike European/American OVHcloud regions where traffic is free
  • Not MeitY empanelled - not eligible for Indian government or regulated PSU procurement
  • No Indian data sovereignty guarantee - OVHcloud is a French company; DPDP Act compliance requires careful assessment
  • Support can be slow - unmanaged products rely on community forums and ticketing; 24/7 live support is limited to premium tiers
  • Price increases in 2026 - VPS 2026 range prices rose 40–55% effective April 2026 due to RAM supply constraints; older ranges remain stable
  • Mumbai DC is relatively new (2023) - smaller footprint than European/Canadian regions; not all products available locally yet

Alternatives to OVHcloud

India's NSE-listed GPU cloud with H200, H100, spot pricing from ₹49/hr, and INR billing. Better than OVHcloud for pure GPU workloads within India - no forex risk, no Mumbai traffic charges.

UTH
Utho→

India's most affordable VPS cloud with NVMe SSDs, INR pricing, and 7 DC locations. More affordable than OVHcloud for India-based VPS; lacks OVHcloud's global reach and dedicated server range.

CYF
Cyfuture Cloud→

MeitY-empanelled Indian cloud with 20+ years of enterprise track record. Better for Indian government and regulated sector work; lacks OVHcloud's global DC footprint and European compliance stack.

ACE
AceCloud→

India's GPU-first cloud with H100, A100 at up to 60% cheaper than AWS. Better for AI/ML teams wanting INR billing and Indian data sovereignty - no global reach.

NEY
Neysa→

Blackstone-backed India neocloud with a full managed AI stack. Better for enterprises needing Indian data sovereignty with premium SLAs. Lacks OVHcloud's global presence and VPS range.

DO

Developer-friendly global cloud with a Bengaluru DC. More polished UX and managed services than OVHcloud; generally pricier for equivalent raw compute but better developer experience.
