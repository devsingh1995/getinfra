About Cyfuture Cloud

Cyfuture is one of India's oldest and most established cloud and data center providers, founded in 2001 by Anuj Bairathi and headquartered in Noida. With over two decades of infrastructure expertise, Cyfuture has grown from a colocation and managed hosting company into a full-spectrum AI cloud platform operating under the brand Cyfuture AI (cyfuture.ai) for AI workloads and Cyfuture Cloud (cyfuture.cloud) for enterprise IaaS.

Cyfuture operates five Tier III data centers in India - Noida (×2), Jaipur, Raipur, and Bengaluru - all MeitY-empanelled. This makes it one of very few Indian providers with both STQC-audited MeitY empanelment for public cloud and government/VPC cloud, covering the full spectrum of government, BFSI, healthcare, and enterprise procurement requirements. Cyfuture AI describes its team as 2,000+ dedicated AI and infrastructure experts serving 10,000+ global clients, including enterprise and Fortune 500 workloads.

The Cyfuture AI platform combines GPU-as-a-Service, inferencing, fine-tuning, RAG, AI IDE Lab, voice AI, AI agents, model library, vector database and cloud storage. Its public pricing page lists INR-based GPU rates and highlights per-hour billing with no platform fees or egress fees.

Unlike newer GPU-only neoclouds, Cyfuture's deep enterprise lineage means it also provides managed colocation, bare metal, VMware-based vApps, Windows and Linux VMs, dedicated servers, CDN, disaster recovery, and BPO services - making it the natural choice for enterprises that want a single trusted Indian vendor for their full IT stack, not just GPU compute.

Cyfuture Cloud Review: Who Should Use It?

Cyfuture Cloud is best suited for Indian enterprises, government-facing projects, BFSI, healthcare, AI teams and managed infrastructure buyers who want Indian cloud infrastructure with GPU, AI services and enterprise support under one provider. It is a stronger fit when the buyer values compliance posture, Noida-based operations, Indian billing and a broader managed IT stack rather than only the lowest self-serve GPU price.

Startups and developers looking for a simple low-cost VPS may compare Cyfuture with Utho or DigitalOcean. AI teams focused mainly on self-serve GPU pricing may also compare E2E Networks, AceCloud and Neysa before finalizing workload placement.

Common questions

Cyfuture AI GPU FAQs

What GPUs and AI accelerators does Cyfuture AI offer?

Cyfuture AI lists NVIDIA H200, H100, A100, L40S and V100, AMD MI300X and MI325X, and Intel Gaudi2 configurations.

What is the lowest listed Cyfuture AI GPU price?

The NVIDIA V100 has the lowest listed on-demand rate at ₹54 per hour. Its published twelve-month reserved rate is ₹39 per hour.

Does Cyfuture AI offer reserved GPU pricing?

Yes. The public pricing page lists one-month, six-month and twelve-month reserved rates alongside on-demand pricing.

Does Cyfuture AI offer spot GPUs?

Spot availability is not presented as a standard column in the public GPU pricing table. Confirm current spot or interruptible capacity directly with Cyfuture AI for your selected accelerator and region.

Which Cyfuture AI GPU is best for LLM inference?

L40S is a practical starting point for cost-sensitive inference. H100, H200, A100, MI300X and MI325X provide more memory or performance for larger models and heavier concurrency.

Does Cyfuture AI support multi-GPU configurations?

Yes. Published configurations for several accelerator families scale from one to two, four and eight devices. Larger managed clusters may require an enterprise arrangement.

Are the prices on this page guaranteed?

No. Pricing, exchange settings, capacity and commercial terms can change. Confirm the final configuration, applicable taxes and current rate directly with Cyfuture AI.
