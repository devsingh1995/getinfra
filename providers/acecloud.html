About AceCloud

AceCloud is an India-based GPU-first cloud platform founded in 2020 and headquartered in Noida. Built specifically for AI, ML, and high-performance compute workloads, it offers enterprise-grade GPU clusters - including NVIDIA H100, A100, and L40S - at prices significantly lower than hyperscalers like AWS and Azure.

Beyond GPU compute, AceCloud also provides NVMe-backed Cloud VPS, managed Kubernetes, object storage, load balancers, and private networking - making it a full-stack Indian cloud for teams that need serious compute without the international price premium.

All pricing is in Indian Rupees with no hidden charges. AceCloud targets AI/ML startups, research labs, and enterprises seeking cost-effective GPU infrastructure within India's data sovereignty boundaries.

✅

Common questions

AceCloud GPU FAQs

What GPUs does AceCloud offer?

AceCloud offers 10 NVIDIA GPU models: H200, H100, A100 80GB, L40S, L4, RTX A6000, RTX 6000 Ada, RTX PRO 6000, A30 and A2. Availability varies by region, configuration and billing type.

How much does an AceCloud GPU cost?

Pricing depends on the GPU, region, GPU count, vCPU, RAM and billing model. Published starting monthly prices range from ₹12,000 for an A2 configuration to ₹2,20,000 for an H200 configuration, before taxes and optional resources.

Does AceCloud offer GPUs in India?

Yes. AceCloud provides India-focused GPU infrastructure with locations including Noida and Mumbai, along with a United States location in Atlanta. Confirm the exact GPU inventory available in your selected location before provisioning.

Is AceCloud suitable for LLM workloads?

It can be. The listed H100, H200, A100, L40S and L4 options cover workloads ranging from efficient inference to large-model training. The right choice depends on model size, precision, context length, concurrency and GPU count.

Does AceCloud provide services beyond GPU instances?

Yes. AceCloud provides virtual servers, managed Kubernetes, block and object storage, networking, load balancing, CDN and managed database services alongside GPU infrastructure.

Are the prices on this page guaranteed?

No. Pricing and availability are comparison snapshots. Rates, inventory, regions, credits and billing terms can change, so verify the final configuration and price directly with AceCloud before launching an instance.
