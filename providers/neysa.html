About Neysa

Neysa is India's first fully AI-focused cloud platform - a "neocloud" founded in 2023 and headquartered in Mumbai. Co-founded by Sharad Sanghi (who previously built Netmagic into India's largest data center company before its NTT acquisition) and Anindya Das, Neysa is designed from the ground up for AI and machine learning workloads, not retrofitted from a general-purpose cloud.

Its flagship product, Neysa Velocis, is a full-stack AI Acceleration Cloud System combining GPU-as-a-Service, AI Platform-as-a-Service, Inference-as-a-Service, Orchestration & MLOps, and the proprietary Aegis LLM Shield - a security layer purpose-built for enterprise AI governance. The platform supports fractional GPU billing, spot-aware scheduling, per-minute billing, and pre-configured AI stacks including PyTorch, TensorFlow, and Hugging Face.

In 2026, Neysa announced funding of over $1 billion, with Blackstone reported as a major backer. The capital is aimed at expanding AI cloud infrastructure and GPU capacity for Indian enterprises, AI-native startups and regulated workloads. Because valuation and funding details can change, buyers should verify the latest corporate information from Neysa's official newsroom or trusted business sources.

Neysa targets enterprises, regulated sectors, AI-native startups and research institutions that need high-performance GPU compute, AI-native tooling, Indian deployment context and managed support. It sits at a higher-service tier compared to self-serve GPU clouds because it bundles infrastructure, platform services, observability and AI governance tooling into one stack.

🧭

Neysa AI Review: Who Should Use It?

Neysa is best suited for enterprises, AI-native startups, BFSI teams, research labs and product teams that want more than raw GPU rental. The platform is useful when a team needs GPU infrastructure, managed AI tooling, inference endpoints, Kubernetes orchestration, monitoring and AI security from one provider.

Cost-sensitive teams that only need basic VPS hosting or self-serve GPU instances should still compare Neysa with E2E Networks, AceCloud, Utho and other cloud providers. Neysa's strongest fit is production AI, managed MLOps, enterprise governance and India-focused AI infrastructure rather than simple commodity compute.

What's good about Neysa

  • Full-stack AI platform (Velocis) - not just raw GPU rental; includes MLOps, observability, model catalog, and inference endpoints
  • Fractional GPU support - share a GPU for smaller workloads, pay only for what you use down to per-minute billing

Neysa AI FAQs

What is Neysa?

Neysa is an AI Acceleration Cloud provider built for AI training, fine-tuning, inference and enterprise AI deployment. Its Velocis AI Cloud platform includes GPU-as-a-Service, Inference-as-a-Service, AI PaaS, orchestration and MLOps, GPU monitoring, AI infrastructure security, LLM Shield, AI catalog and marketplace features.

Is Neysa an Indian company?

Yes. Neysa is an India-based AI cloud infrastructure company. Neysa’s official contact page lists Neysa Networks Private Limited with offices in Mumbai, Bengaluru and Chennai. The company’s official press release also says Neysa designs and develops AI systems that are deployed and operated within India.

Who founded Neysa?

Neysa was founded by Sharad Sanghi and Anindya Das. Sharad Sanghi is the Co-Founder and CEO of Neysa, and Anindya Das is the Co-Founder and CTO. The founding team has deep data center and cloud infrastructure experience, including previous leadership experience associated with Netmagic and NTT.

What is Neysa Velocis?

Neysa Velocis is Neysa’s AI cloud platform for building, deploying, scaling and monitoring AI workloads. It supports managed GPU VM instances, bare-metal GPUs, managed Kubernetes clusters, inference endpoints, MLOps workflows and AI infrastructure monitoring from a single platform.

What is Neysa GPU pricing?

Neysa’s official pricing page lists GPU pricing in USD. Current listed rates include NVIDIA L4 starting at $1.17/hour, L40S at $1.95/hour, H100 SXM at $4.39/hour, H100 NVL at $4.39/hour and H200 SXM at $4.73/hour. Neysa also offers reserved monthly pricing, so final costs may vary by GPU type, usage hours, commitment term, deployment model and enterprise configuration.

What GPUs does Neysa offer?

Neysa lists NVIDIA L4, L40S, H100 SXM, H100 NVL and H200 SXM GPUs on its official pricing page. These GPUs support AI inference, model fine-tuning, training, high-throughput workloads, enterprise AI applications and GPU-accelerated development environments.

Does Neysa offer bare-metal GPUs?

Yes. Neysa’s pricing page lists Bare Metal GPUs as one of its deployment options. It describes bare-metal GPUs as single-tenant servers suited for heavy training workloads, high-throughput performance, exclusive GPU access and multi-GPU nodes on request.

Does Neysa offer managed Kubernetes?

Yes. Neysa offers Managed Kubernetes Clusters with master and worker node options. Its pricing page positions managed Kubernetes for teams that want to deploy and orchestrate AI workloads at scale using containers and AI pipelines.

Is Neysa funded by Blackstone?

Yes. Neysa announced a $1.2 billion capital raise led by Blackstone and co-investors. The funding includes up to $600 million in equity from Blackstone and co-investors, with Neysa planning to secure an additional $600 million in debt financing.

Is Neysa listed on the stock market?

No. Neysa is not listed on the stock market. Neysa Networks Private Limited is an unlisted private company, so there is no official Neysa share price on NSE or BSE.

Where is Neysa located?

Neysa’s official contact page lists offices in Mumbai, Bengaluru and Chennai. The Mumbai office is listed under Neysa Networks Private Limited at Art Guild House, Phoenix Marketcity, Kurla West, Mumbai.

What are the best Neysa alternatives?

Common Neysa alternatives include E2E Networks, AceCloud, Cyfuture Cloud, Utho, AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Lambda Labs and CoreWeave. Neysa is a strong fit for AI-focused teams that need GPU cloud, managed Kubernetes, inference infrastructure and enterprise AI infrastructure in India. Other providers may be better depending on budget, GPU availability, managed support, global regions, compliance requirements and self-serve deployment needs.

Alternatives to Neysa

Compare Neysa against other cloud providers:

E2E
E2E Networks→

NSE-listed Indian cloud with public GPU pricing and strong H100/H200 availability. More self-serve and often lower cost than Neysa, but with less managed AI tooling.

ACE
AceCloud→

Indian GPU cloud provider with H100, A100 and L40S options at competitive INR pricing. Good alternative for teams that want GPU infrastructure with managed support but do not need Neysa's full AI platform layer.

UTH
Utho→

India's most affordable VPS cloud with GPU options. Best for general-purpose workloads and budget-conscious teams who don't need Neysa's enterprise AI stack.

CYF
Cyfuture Cloud→

MeitY-empanelled Indian cloud with strong government and enterprise lineage. Similar compliance posture to Neysa but less AI-native; good for traditional cloud workloads.

AWS
Amazon AWS→

Global hyperscaler with Mumbai and Hyderabad regions. Deepest ML service ecosystem (SageMaker, Bedrock) but often more complex and expensive for GPU-heavy workloads, with different data residency and service-control trade-offs.

DO
DigitalOcean→

Developer-friendly global cloud with a Bengaluru DC. Strong DX and docs but no Indian-sovereign GPU cluster. Better for web apps than AI training workloads.
