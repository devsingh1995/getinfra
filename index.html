FAQs

Cloud Pricing Comparison FAQs

Quick answers for buyers comparing VPS, GPU cloud and infrastructure providers in India.

What is getInfra.cloud?+

getInfra.cloud is an independent cloud infrastructure comparison platform for Indian buyers comparing VPS plans, GPU cloud pricing, data center locations, bandwidth, support and provider fit.

Is getInfra.cloud affiliated with any cloud provider?+

No. getInfra.cloud is not affiliated with the listed providers and does not rank providers based on commissions, referral fees or sponsorships.

How often is pricing updated?+

Pricing is reviewed regularly. The current homepage update marker is 2026. Always verify final pricing on the provider website before purchasing.

Are prices shown with GST included?+

GST and tax treatment can vary by provider, billing location and account setup. The table flags GST status where possible and recommends direct verification.

Can I compare GPU cloud pricing in India?+

Yes. The GPU pricing section helps users compare providers for AI training, inference, fine-tuning, RAG and development workloads.

Should I choose an Indian cloud provider or a global hyperscaler?+

Choose based on workload, region, support needs, compliance requirements, pricing model and ecosystem fit. Indian providers may be attractive for INR billing, local support and data residency, while hyperscalers may offer broader global services.
